About

I'm a Ph.D. candidate in Materials Science and Engineering at POSTECH, advised by Prof. Kyu-Young Park in the Energy Materials Research Laboratory (EMRL).

My research explores how local atomic structure and disorder influence the electrochemical behavior of lithium-ion battery cathodes. I combine first-principles calculations and atomistic modeling to study Li-rich layered oxides and disordered rocksalt materials. My interests include:

  • Disorder & oxygen redox: local cation arrangements, oxygen redox, and structural evolution in Li-rich cathodes [J2]
  • Atomistic modeling: first-principles calculations, cluster expansion, and Monte Carlo simulations
  • Machine learning interatomic potentials: first-principles accuracy at larger length and time scales
  • Large-scale data analysis & statistics for materials datasets
  • Electrode structure & transport: how particle arrangement and processing affect battery performance [J1]

I'm always open to collaborations. Feel free to reach out by email.
