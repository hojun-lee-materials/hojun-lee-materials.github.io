ABOUT ME

Understanding disorder
in battery materials.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Materials Science and Engineering at POSTECH, advised by Prof. Kyu-Young Park in the Energy Materials Research Laboratory (EMRL).

My research explores how local atomic structure and disorder influence the electrochemical behavior of lithium-ion battery cathodes. I combine first-principles calculations and atomistic modeling to study Li-rich layered oxides and disordered rocksalt materials. My interests also include machine learning interatomic potentials, large-scale data analysis and statistics, and the links between electrode structure, transport, and battery performance.
